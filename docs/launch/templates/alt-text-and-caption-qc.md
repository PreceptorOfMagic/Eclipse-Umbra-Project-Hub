QC:

- [ ] Alt text describes the actual delivery, not the planned shot.
- [ ] It does not infer emotion, identity, performance or independence that the
  pixels do not establish.
- [ ] Important visible text is transcribed; decorative text is omitted.
- [ ] Adjacent prose is not repeated word for word.
- [ ] The caption labels a diagram/recreated view and never presents it as live proof.
- [ ] Filename, source ID/frame, rights record and privacy review agree.

## Caption-file review

- [ ] Every spoken word needed for meaning is present and speakers are identified only when necessary.
- [ ] Meaningful sounds use concise square-bracket descriptions.
- [ ] Cues do not overlap, disappear before the relevant sound ends, or exceed comfortable reading speed.
- [ ] On-screen credit names were checked against `ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md`.
- [ ] Captions were reviewed in signed-out playback on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] The final cue ends at or before the hosted film duration in `media-manifest.json`.
