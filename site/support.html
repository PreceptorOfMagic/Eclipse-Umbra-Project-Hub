Why this route

Buy Me a Coffee gives the project one recognisable checkout instead of several competing buttons. Supporters can choose a one-time payment or no-strings-attached monthly support, while Stripe handles identity verification and the Australian payout account. The link above goes directly to the verified creator page.

Does the maintainer need an ABN?

Not merely to receive a payment. Australian Government guidance says an ABN is not compulsory for every business, and a hobby is not entitled to one. Stripe's published Connect requirements vary with the platform, capabilities and entity type; an accurately classified Australian individual account may not show an ABN as a standard required field, while Stripe says an ABN is typically required for an Australian unincorporated entity.

The answer changes if the activity is really a sole-trader business or another unincorporated entity. An ABN may also be needed for GST or to register a business name. The onboarding category must match the facts; “free” and “open source” do not automatically make an activity a hobby, charity or legally recognised non-profit. A completed Stripe connection confirms onboarding, not the account's unseen classification or its legal ABN position.

Stripe's Australian agreement separately limits its services to business purposes rather than personal, family or household use. That processor-contract requirement is distinct from statutory ABN entitlement: the real creator activity must fit both the platform contract and its correct Australian tax/legal classification.

Tax and GST

Calling a payment a tip does not make it tax-free. Buy Me a Coffee says support is typically subject to income tax, and ATO guidance asks why and in what capacity a receipt was earned. The working record policy is therefore conservative: keep the gross payment, platform and processor fees, refunds, disputes, payout, original currency, AUD conversion and bank entry separately—not only the net amount that reaches the bank.

A genuinely reward-free tip is likely not consideration for a GST supply because the supporter receives no goods, services or promised action. Rewards, memberships, advertising, feature commitments or special access could change that. If an enterprise reaches the A$75,000 current or projected GST-turnover threshold, GST registration generally becomes mandatory and requires an ABN first.

Queensland's Collections Act can also apply to an appeal for a Minister-determined community purpose. Whether this page itself falls into that category is unresolved, and the Act assigns the determination to the Minister; personal or discretionary wording is not a legal safe harbour. The project does not claim charitable status or a ring-fenced community fund. A written Queensland OFT classification remains an open action before broad public promotion.

Fees, payouts and reversals

The creator page uses USD as its base currency and Buy Me a Coffee may show a local conversion to visitors. Buy Me a Coffee currently publishes a 5% platform fee, 2.9% + USD 0.30 processing and a 0.5% payout fee, with another 1% for international transactions and 0.5% for recurring payments where applicable. Its schedule differs from ordinary Australian Stripe pricing, so the two schedules are not added together; transaction records will preserve the actual connected-account treatment.

The first payout is manually reviewed, starts once the balance reaches the platform's stated USD 10 minimum and can take roughly 7–14 days. Support is ordinarily non-refundable, but duplicate, unauthorised or reviewed transactions may be reversed. Chargebacks can remove the payment and add a dispute fee. Monthly supporters can cancel future renewals at any time through Buy Me a Coffee.

Payment details at a glance

  • Legal recipient: the Australian individual operating the PreceptorOfMagic creator page; Eclipse and Umbra are application names, not separate entities.
  • Purpose: a discretionary tip recognising published work and helping make continued maintenance and testing practical; no particular expense or future feature is promised.
  • Fees: Buy Me a Coffee's published 5% platform fee plus its stated processing and payout charges; the connected account records the actual amount.
  • Currency: USD base currency, with localised display or conversion where Buy Me a Coffee offers it.
  • Recurring: supporters may choose one-time or monthly support; neither option carries perks or access.
  • Cancellation: monthly supporters can stop future renewals through Buy Me a Coffee; the creator can also cancel them.
  • Refund: support is ordinarily non-refundable, subject to platform review for duplicate, unauthorised or other accepted cases.
  • Privacy: Buy Me a Coffee and Stripe process data and may transfer or store it outside Australia. The creator may receive supporter details such as a chosen name, email, postal address or message; the maintainer keeps only what is needed for support, disputes and compliance and does not reuse it for marketing.
  • Tax: the recipient handles Australian reporting; this is not a tax-deductible donation and no DGR receipt is issued.
  • Contact: use the Buy Me a Coffee page or its support channel for payment questions; use the project issue trackers for software issues.
  • Closure: closing the creator page stops future support; prior tips remain with the individual recipient and no ring-fenced project balance is represented.

The support promise

  • The software, source, documentation, releases and issue trackers remain available without payment.
  • A tip buys no feature, delivery date, vote, priority support, early access, private build or technical influence.
  • The recipient is not presented as a charity or Australian deductible gift recipient and will not issue DGR receipts.
  • Supporters will not be added to marketing. Any future mailing list would require a separate express opt-in and unsubscribe path.
  • Payment data stays with Buy Me a Coffee and Stripe where practical. They may process it outside Australia, and any supporter details received by the maintainer are retained only for support, disputes and compliance.
  • Funding never changes the GPL or other third-party licence obligations.

Disclosure beside the button

Eclipse/Umbra is free, open-source software. If the published work has been useful, you can optionally leave a one-time or monthly tip for its individual maintainer through Buy Me a Coffee. The tip is voluntary, is not earmarked for a promised feature and buys no goods, services, access, priority, exclusive content or influence. The recipient may use it at their discretion, including to make continued maintenance and testing practical.

The recipient is not an Australian deductible gift recipient. We do not issue DGR receipts or represent support as an Australian deductible gift. Buy Me a Coffee and Stripe process the payment and deduct their applicable fees. Support is ordinarily non-refundable, subject to platform review for matters such as duplicate or unauthorised payments; monthly support can be cancelled for future renewals.

The recipient is the Australian individual operating the linked creator page; the base currency, published fee treatment and contact route are stated above without exposing unnecessary identity documents. Payments go to that individual, not to Moonlight, Sunshine, Moonlight TV, Aurora, Apollo, LG Electronics, NVIDIA, AMD or their maintainers.
