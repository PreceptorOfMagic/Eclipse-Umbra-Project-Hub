What the final page must prove

The accepted video will show two independently controlled live sessions from two Umbra hosts, composed by one Eclipse client on a shared display. It will include isolated input from each player, simultaneous movement, the real host/layout/input picker and both layout orientations.

Transcript

The final spoken words, meaningful sound cues and speaker changes will be copied from the reviewed coop-walkthrough-transcript.md source only after the film is locked.

Visual description

A shot-by-shot description will identify what each pane, player action, control screen, layout transition and credit card visibly demonstrates. It will distinguish diagrams from live footage and will not infer performance claims from the edit.

Recording evidence

The public record will identify the release versions and artifact hashes used without exposing machine names, addresses, pairing codes or other private rig details. Rights and consent records remain with the private production record.

Credits

The finished page will retain the creator-first credit shown in the film and link to the complete acknowledgements and licence inventory.
