Everyday streaming

A complete client before co-op begins.

Eclipse descends from Moonlight TV and Aurora and continues to provide the expected host discovery, secure pairing, app selection, live video, low-delay audio and input path.

Picture

Decoder-aware choices from 720p through 4K, native and custom modes, separate 30–240 fps controls, automatic or manual bitrate, H.264 and HEVC, plus AV1 and HDR10 where the platform can decode them.

Sound

Low-delay stereo and multichannel playback for ordinary sessions. Co-op can mix both stereo hosts or keep one selected source.

Controls

Gamepad, keyboard and mouse input; on-screen keyboard; virtual mouse; and capability-aware rumble, trigger rumble, touchpad, gyro, accelerometer and RGB forwarding where supported.

Visibility

An in-stream performance overlay exposes the negotiated codec, size, frame rate, round-trip time, drops, bitrate and processing latency. Built-in diagnostics create a reportable record when a session fails.

Co-op composer

Two live sessions become one playable view.

Eclipse coordinates two paired Umbra hosts, chooses the compatible stream contract, presents either host as Player 1, and keeps the controls for both PCs explicit.
