# Support

## Getting help

If something fails, open an [Eclipse issue](https://github.com/PreceptorOfMagic/Eclipse/issues/new/choose) or an [Umbra issue](https://github.com/PreceptorOfMagic/Umbra/issues/new/choose)—or reply in the project’s community thread—with the exact OS, GPU, driver, client and display. Website and documentation problems belong in this repository’s issues.

[Eclipse’s Share logs feature](docs/installation.md#diagnostics) packages system capabilities, settings, session events, performance totals, host logs and crash or unclean-exit records so a report can be investigated without guesswork. Review the bundle for personal information before attaching it. The [testing matrix](README.md#status) shows which hardware has been exercised.

## Supporting the project

Eclipse/Umbra is free, open-source software. If the published work has been useful, you can optionally leave a one-time or monthly tip for its individual maintainer through [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/preceptorofmagic). The tip is voluntary, is not earmarked for a promised feature and buys no goods, services, access, priority, exclusive content or influence. The recipient may use it at their discretion, including to make continued maintenance and testing practical.

The recipient is not an Australian deductible gift recipient. We do not issue DGR receipts or represent support as an Australian deductible gift. Buy Me a Coffee and Stripe process the payment and deduct the fees shown at checkout or in the provider’s current schedule. The page uses USD as its base currency and can show a local conversion. Paid support is ordinarily non-refundable, subject to platform review for matters such as duplicate or unauthorised payments; a monthly supporter can cancel future renewals.

Payments go to the Australian individual who operates the creator page. They do not go to Moonlight, Sunshine, Moonlight TV, Aurora, Apollo, LG Electronics, NVIDIA, AMD or their maintainers. To support an upstream project, follow the funding links on that project’s own repository or maintainer profile; direct upstream links are in [ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md](ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS.md).

Buy Me a Coffee and Stripe process supporter and recipient data under their own terms and privacy policies, and may transfer or store it overseas.
